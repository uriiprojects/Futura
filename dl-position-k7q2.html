    How to read this page

    IRR (annual effective rate)The yearly return that takes into account when each peso went in and when it comes back. Two partners who earn the same profit can have very different IRRs if one waited longer.
    MultiplePesos you get back per peso invested. 1.30x means you receive $1.30 for every $1.00 you put in.
    Slot (cupo)An equal share of the capital the project needs beyond the land. There are 5 slots in Casa Trama; each funds about 14% of the total cost and earns profit pro rata.
    Pro rataEveryone’s payout is proportional to what they contributed to the project’s total cost. Land and slot pesos earn the same multiple.
    Futura fee15% of the profit, charged only if there is a profit. Closing costs (5% of the sale price) are deducted before profit is calculated.
    ScenariosConservative, Base and Upside are three sale prices. Everything else (costs, dates) stays the same unless you edit it.

    Every payment and receipt, in order

    Negative = money you pay in. Positive = money you receive. This is exactly the cash flow the IRRs are calculated from.

    The full deal: how each sale is split

    Same rule in both projects: closing costs come off the sale price; Futura takes 15% of the profit; everything left (the cost plus 85% of the profit) goes back to the partners pro rata to what each one contributed.

    What a slower project does to returns · Casa Trama

    Delays apply to Casa Trama only (construction start and sale). Cantaluna keeps its actual contributions and its sale date. The money is the same; what changes is how long it waited: a slower project does not reduce the profit in pesos, it reduces the IRR.
